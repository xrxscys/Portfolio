My primary professional work is Ulta Hub at CSAM Philippines — a SaaS platform for Virtual Assistant workforce management, currently in final testing before going live. I started as an intern and moved into a project-based role as the platform scaled. Angular on the front, ASP.NET Core on the back, SignalR in between — across a role-based permissions system spanning 40+ modules, live geolocation via Azure Maps, real-time timesheets, and a rich-text chat system backed by 9 real-time entities.

Before that, I built EduOps — a school management system for an actual institutional client during university. Student financial ledgers, enrollment tracking, grading modules, a full notification system with email and in-app delivery. It had to work for real people depending on it.

Recent graduate of the University of San Carlos — B.S. in Information Technology, GWA 1.93 / 1.0. I work best when the problem has real stakes and the team has high standards. Frontend-heavy, full-stack, or anywhere clean architecture and precise UI both matter.
